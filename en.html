About me

I'm Tiffany, a taxi driver on Ischia. 💙 Every day, behind the wheel of my little micro-taxi, I take people from all over the world to discover the island.

And you know what made me smile the most? Before leaving, so many people told me: "I wish I could take a piece of Ischia home with me." 🌊☀️

So I thought: why not create something that brings that moment back to life every time you look at it? That's how my little Tiffany gadget collection was born: simple, colourful, original pieces inspired by Ischia, my micro-taxi, and those days you wish would never end. 🛵💎

A t-shirt to wear, and be back at the sea in your mind. A keychain to keep with you always. A fan to feel that island breeze again. A lighter, a pen… small details that tell one big feeling: Ischia. ❤️

Because a trip ends when you get home. A keepsake, though, can stay with you every day.

Want to take a piece of Ischia with you? Message me directly on WhatsApp and choose your Tiffany gadget: I'll reply personally! 💬✨

Fundraiser: helping Mario and Maria Grazia

Mario and Maria Grazia are neighbours of mine here on Ischia. In July 2026 their only home, built by their parents, was demolished over a bureaucratic process that has dragged on for decades. On top of having to pay rent, they also found themselves covering the cost of the demolition itself, at over 60 years of age. I decided to put my gadgets to work for them, because anyone who's lived on this island their whole life deserves real help.

The full proceeds from every product I sell, minus only the production cost, go to Mario and Maria Grazia. The fundraiser is always active, with no deadline.
